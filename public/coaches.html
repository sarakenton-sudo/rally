Questions coaches ask

FAQ

Do my families need the app?

No. They can book from your link in any browser. The RallyHUB app is free for them too, and it also runs their tournament season: schedules, hotels, team codes and streaming links, so your lessons sit right next to everything else they check every weekend.

How do I get paid?

The way you already do: cash, Venmo or Zelle. Mark each lesson paid in two taps and RallyHUB keeps track of what's collected and what's still owed, so nothing slips through.

What if they pay cash?

That's fine. Open the lesson, tap Mark paid and pick cash, Venmo, Zelle or other. Today and your earnings show what's been collected and what's still outstanding.

Can I keep using my own calendar?

Yes. Add your RallyHUB lessons to Google Calendar (or Apple or Outlook) with one link, and they stay in sync as lessons are booked, moved or cancelled.

What do families sign?

Your lesson terms and a liability release, plus allergies and an emergency contact. Use the RallyHUB default or customize it. Families sign once on their phone, it stays in their file, and they're asked again only if you change your terms.

Can I run groups, clinics and camps too?

Yes. Set up private, semi-private, small group, clinic and camp sessions with their own price, length and number of spots.
